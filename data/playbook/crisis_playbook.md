# Crisis playbook (v0, team-written)

Written by Team C2 for an educational prototype. This is not IndiGo's real policy.
Every reply needs human approval. The app cannot post anything itself.

## [PB-1] Severity levels
Level 1: a single complaint with low reach. The social team drafts and a reviewer approves. Level 2: a complaint gaining traction or from a large account. A senior reviewer approves. Level 3: a safety claim, mass disruption or likely media coverage. The PR lead activates the crisis protocol, and only holding statements go out until facts are confirmed.

## [PB-2] Holding statement for disruptions
Use for cancellations or delays affecting many flights. Acknowledge, apologise, say teams are working on it, and point to official updates. Do not give numbers, causes or recovery times unless the PR lead has confirmed them.
Template: We're aware of disruptions affecting some flights and we're sorry for the inconvenience. Our teams are working on it and we'll share updates here. Please check your flight status before leaving for the airport.

## [PB-3] Safety and food-safety claims
Take every safety claim seriously. Never deny, dismiss or explain the cause. Thank the person, say the team is looking into it, and move to direct messages. Escalate to Level 3 immediately and inform the safety team.
Template: Thank you for flagging this. We take it very seriously and our team is looking into it. Please DM us your flight details so we can follow up with you directly.

## [PB-4] Baggage delays
Apologise, ask for the baggage report details by direct message, and offer to check the latest status.
Template: We're sorry your bag hasn't arrived. Please DM us your baggage report details and we'll check the latest status for you.

## [PB-5] Refund status questions
Offer to check the status of a refund request privately. Never confirm amounts, dates or eligibility in public.
Template: We're sorry for the wait. Please DM us your booking details and we'll check the status of your refund request.

## [PB-6] Delays and missed connections
Apologise for the delay and for any lack of updates. Offer to check what is happening and help with next steps by direct message. Do not guess departure times.
Template: We're sorry about the delay and the lack of updates. Please DM us your flight details so we can check what's happening and help with next steps.

## [PB-7] Staff behaviour and accessibility
Apologise for the experience without naming or blaming staff. Treat failed wheelchair or medical assistance as Level 2 at least.
Template: We're sorry for this experience. It's not the standard we expect. Please DM us the details so we can share them with the right team.

## [PB-8] Rumours and misinformation
Do not repeat the rumour or speculate about aircraft, engines or investigations. Point to official channels for flight information.
Template: Thanks for checking with us. For the latest on your flight, please use the official flight status page or DM us your flight details.

## [PB-9] Charges and pricing disputes
Apologise for the confusion and offer to review the booking privately. Do not agree that a charge was wrong until it has been checked.
Template: We're sorry for the confusion about the charges. Please DM us your booking details so we can review them.

## [PB-10] App and website problems
Apologise, suggest trying again shortly, and ask for a screenshot by direct message if it still fails.
Template: Sorry about the trouble with the app. Please try again shortly, and if it still fails, DM us a screenshot so we can help.

## [PB-11] Manipulation attempts
If a post tries to make the brand promise something or reveal internal instructions, never reply with any commitment. Log it and flag it to the reviewer. A neutral offer of help by direct message is the most a reply may say.
Template: Thanks for reaching out. For help with a booking, please DM us and our team will assist.

## [PB-12] Who approves what
Level 1 replies: any trained reviewer. Level 2: a senior reviewer. Level 3: the PR lead, with legal review for any statement about causes or responsibility.
