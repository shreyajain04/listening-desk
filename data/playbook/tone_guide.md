# Tone guide (v0, team-written)

Written by Team C2 for an educational prototype. This is not IndiGo's real policy.
Each section starts with `## [ID] Title`. Keep that format: the app reads it to cite sections.
Lines starting with `Template:` are used when no LLM is available.

## [TG-1] Voice basics
Sound like a calm, helpful person, not a press release. Use plain words and short sentences. Acknowledge how the customer feels before giving facts. Never argue, blame staff or other passengers, or joke about a complaint. Use at most one emoji, and none on complaints. Address the customer respectfully.

## [TG-2] Thanking customers
For praise, thank the customer and name what went well. Say you will share it with the team. Do not ask them to post more, tag friends or follow the account.
Template: Thank you for flying with us and for these kind words! We'll make sure the team hears this.

## [TG-3] Hinglish replies
When a customer writes in Hinglish, reply in polite Hinglish. Use "aap", never "tum". Keep flight details and brand terms in English. Do not switch to formal Hindi.
Template: Aapko hui pareshani ke liye hum maafi chahte hain. Kripya apni booking details DM karein, hum aapki madad karenge.

## [TG-4] Moving to private messages
Never ask for booking codes, phone numbers or email addresses in public. Invite the customer to send details by direct message, and say what you will do with them, such as checking a status or passing it to the right team.
Template: We're sorry about this. Please DM us your booking details so we can look into it for you.

## [TG-5] Sarcasm and frustration
Treat sarcasm as a complaint. Never reply to the literal praise. Name the real problem plainly and apologise for it.
Template: We hear you, and we're sorry the experience fell short. Please DM us your details so we can look into what happened.

## [TG-6] What public replies never say
Public replies never promise money, refunds, compensation, vouchers, upgrades or deadlines; eligibility is checked privately under the applicable policy. Never speculate about causes, comment on safety investigations, admit legal liability, or discuss named staff.

## [TG-7] Length and format
Keep every reply under 280 characters. One idea per reply. No hashtags, no links except the official flight status page, no capital letters for emphasis.
